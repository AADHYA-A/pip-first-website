My Story (scroll, little one)

  1. 1 · Born under a quilt of stars

    Pip began as a warm glow in a very quiet nursery sky. The glow got round. Then it got a nightcap. Nobody knows who knitted it.

  2. 2 · Hired by the Moon

    The Moon needed someone small enough to fit inside a dream. Pip applied by giggling. Pip got the job.

  3. 3 · The first (clumsy) night

    Pip tripped over a lullaby and tangled in a nightlight cord. Three sleepy ghosts all received the same dream: one giant pancake. They woke up delighted.

  4. 4 · Right now

    Every night Pip flies the rooftops, delivering dreams. Tonight's shift is below. Keep scrolling and watch the route light up.
